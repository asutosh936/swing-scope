Manage it by the rules

  • Time stop: if it hasn't worked within 15 trading days, close it. Dead money is still money at risk.
  • Take profit into resistance: don't wait for the target if price stalls at a level you already identified on the chart. Partial exits are fine.
  • Trail the stop only after the trade has moved meaningfully in your favour — never widen a stop, ever, for any reason.
  • Check earnings before you place the order. Inside 3 days, skip it.
  • Log this trade in the journal when you plan it, not after it closes.
